# R5-1: Physical-limits research spike

## Goal
Replace the "honest limits" assumptions with numbers and procedures. No agent can touch the printer, so this card produces (a) a **kinematic/timing simulator** and (b) a **hardware test protocol** the human runs once, whose structured results feed back into machine profiles.

## Questions to answer with evidence
1. **Geometry:** usable XY area with a pen mount (offset, endstop reach, clip collisions, gantry/Y-carriage limits), paper placement options (A5, A4 in two passes, alignment between passes, error budget between pass 1 and pass 2).
2. **Z-touch:** how to find pen-contact height safely: step-and-check with spring flexure (what signal? paper marking, resistance via the Marlin probe pin, sound, camera side view), auto bed-level mesh interplay (`G29` data, `M420`), maximum pen force vs paper tear, bed tilt compensation per stroke (use the mesh to adjust Z-write height by XY).
3. **Pen lift cost:** Z feedrate/acceleration limits for the T8 leadscrew, time per lift, effect of lift height vs ink smear; strategy to minimise lifts (stroke merging, ligatures) and the time/quality tradeoff curve.
4. **Motion quality:** Marlin junction deviation / acceleration / jerk effects on small letters; maximum useful feedrate for 2-3 mm tall glyphs; corner rounding; recommended `M204/M205` writing profile; arcs vs segments (`G2/G3`, arc welder) and planner buffer limits over USB serial.
5. **Mount and pen:** flex, repeatability, pen types (gel/rollerball/fineliner) and spring rate; whether we should ship our own parametric mount design (OpenSCAD, MIT) instead of redistributing PLTR V2 (no licence).
6. **Marlin specifics:** which G-code subset to use, safe preamble/postamble (`G28`, heaters/fans off, `M84` policy), `M117/M118` progress, resume strategy after disconnect (which line was last executed: `M114` sync, `M110` line numbers/checksums), power-loss behaviour.
7. **Failure modes:** pen runs dry, pen drops, paper shifts, serial drop, thermal runaway protection triggers due to disabled heaters (check), endstop hit.

## Deliverables
1. `docs/developer/research/R5-1.md` with sources (Marlin docs, Creality specs; verify and quote with dates).
2. `research/R5-1/` motion simulator: models acceleration, junction deviation, Z speed, planner buffer; input G-code, output time estimate, per-segment speed, and a trajectory/velocity plot; validated against published Marlin behaviour with unit tests of the planner math (trapezoid profiles).
3. **Hardware test protocol** `docs/user/hardware-test-protocol.md` (the human runs it): step-by-step with safety, each step produces a number or photo; and a JSON schema + validator for the results file `~/.penpress/hardware-results.json` (bed offsets, Z-contact height, backlash/repeatability, max reliable feedrate, lift time, pass-to-pass alignment error, paper size fit). Include printable test patterns generated as G-code.
4. A first **Ender 3 Pro machine profile** (`machine-profiles/ender3pro.toml`) with sourced defaults and every field annotated "measured" or "assumed".
5. ADR `docs/developer/adr/0005-physical-limits.md`: decisions on A4 two-pass, Z-touch method, motion profile, mount strategy.

## Acceptance
- Simulator unit tests cover trapezoid planning, junction speeds, corner cases (zero-length, tiny segments); time estimate within 10% of reference calculations from Marlin math for 5 documented examples.
- Protocol validated by a dry run in the simulator and a schema validator that rejects incomplete/impossible results (negative tests).
- ADR accepted; every machine-profile field has a source or an explicit "assumed, verify with protocol step N".
- Pass-to-pass A4 alignment error budget computed from stated repeatability and shown as a figure; decision made whether two-pass A4 is supported in v0.1.

## Anti-shortcut
- Do not copy numbers from forum posts without quoting source and date.
- Do not claim camera Z-detection works without a modelled signal and measured failure cases (cross-check with R3-1).
- Do not leave an assumption unlabeled.

## Evidence
Commands, simulator validation numbers, protocol dry-run output, schema negative-test output, ADR link.
