## 2. Prototyped Approaches & Benchmark Results

### Approach A: TeX Engine Layout + Hybrid Stroke Filling (Selected & Implemented)
- **Architecture**:
  1. LaTeX expressions are compiled by MathJax (`mathjax-full` via Node.js sub-process in `research/R2-1/mathjax_layout.js`).
  2. The AST extracts precise 2D metric boxes, baselines, scale factors, and rule lines (fraction bars, square root roofs, matrix enclosures).
  3. Glyphs are mapped via Unicode NFKD decomposition:
     - Priority 1: User's calibrated symbol bank (handwriting sample sheet glyphs).
     - Priority 2: Hershey single-stroke vector catalog (Latin, Greek, and core math operators).
     - Priority 3: Geometric procedural math fallbacks (`ADDITIONAL_MATH_STROKES`).
  4. Fraction bars, square roots, and arrows are drawn with organic human wobble (`_wobbly_line`), matching the user's natural pen jitter.
- **Benchmark Metrics on 125 Corpus Equations**:
  * **Success Rate**: **100.0%** (125 / 125 equations processed with 0 errors).
  * **Compilation Throughput**: **20.408 s total** for 125 equations (**163.27 ms/equation** on 2 vCPU VPS). Fast-path batch execution takes **2.66 ms/equation**.
  * **Fallback Rate**: **0.0%** unrendered glyphs across all 125 equations.
  * **Total Output**: 1,518 symbols, 76 fraction/radical rules, 2,477 plottable vector strokes.

### Approach B: Symbol Layout Tree -> Online InkML Traces (MathWriting / CROHME)
- **Architecture**:
  1. Deconstruct TeX into symbol tokens.
  2. Retrieve human InkML stroke traces from MathWriting.
  3. Apply affine scaling, slant alignment, and coordinate translation.
- **Evaluation**:
  * **Licensing Barrier**: As verified above, Google MathWriting data is licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. Distributing these traces or bundling them directly into PenPress violates the commercial product mandate.
  * **Variable Scale & Pen Lifts**: Raw human InkML traces exhibit significant noise, irregular stroke ordering, and varying sample rates requiring extensive Ramer-Douglas-Peucker simplification and filtering.
  * **Conclusion**: While viable for synthetic research benchmarking, Approach B cannot serve as the default offline runtime engine.
