## 1. Goal and Method Overview

The vision pipeline enables camera-assisted setup for Ender 3 Pro pen plotting without requiring manual coordinate measurement. From a single webcam or smartphone frame, the system:
1. Detects paper quadrilateral boundaries and orientation.
2. Identifies binder clips and tape obstacles (producing physical keep-out zones).
3. Computes the 3x3 homography matrix mapping image pixels to physical bed coordinates (in mm).
4. Estimates pen nozzle offset from a test calibration mark.
5. Performs post-plot ink presence verification.

Crucially, the system features a **strict self-diagnostic failure taxonomy**: it detects when imaging conditions are adverse (glare, dark shadow, corner clipping, boundary touches) and rejects the frame with high confidence, routing the user to the manual setup wizard rather than producing silently corrupt toolpaths.

## 5. Camera Placement Guide for Users

1. **Optimal Angle:** 0 deg (top-down) to 25 deg oblique facing the front of the bed. Beyond 40 deg, foreshortening compresses aspect ratio and increases corner uncertainty.
2. **Lighting:** Diffuse indirect room light or desk lamp. Avoid direct point-source LEDs shining perpendicular to glossy paper to prevent specular glare occlusion.
3. **Paper Margins:** Maintain at least 15 mm of dark bed plate visible around all 4 paper edges (ensures complete contour closure).
4. **Phone-as-Camera Flow:** Accessed via `http://100.121.213.19:<port>` or loopback over private Tailscale network. Zero video stream egress.
