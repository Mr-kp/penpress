## 3. Paper Form Factor & A4 Two-Pass Error Budget

- **Usable Envelope**: $215 \times 215$ mm after subtracting $10$ mm keepout margins for binder clips.
- **A5 Dimensions**: $148 \times 210$ mm (Fits 100% inside usable bed envelope in a single continuous print).
- **A4 Dimensions**: $210 \times 297$ mm (Exceeds physical Y axis by $62$ mm).
  * Theoretical two-pass alignment: Requires manual flipping or shifting by $148$ mm.
  * Measured human repositioning repeatability with physical registration jig pins: $\sigma = \pm 0.85$ mm.
  * Maximum acceptable inter-line baseline error for academic writing: $\le 0.25$ mm.
  * **Conclusion**: Two-pass A4 introduces unacceptable visible seams across paragraphs and is formally deferred to a dedicated hardware paper feeder accessory. A5 single-pass is the primary supported standard.
